      How routes are computed. Distance is the Manhattan grid distance, |x2 - x1| + |y2 - y1|, in km, on the place grid. Speeds: drive 30 km/h, transit 20 km/h plus a 5 min wait, walk 5 km/h, cycle 15 km/h. Time in minutes is distance / speed * 60, rounded to the nearest minute, plus the wait for transit. The alternative route distance is the main distance x 1.3, rounded to 1 decimal, because it follows the scenic river road.
