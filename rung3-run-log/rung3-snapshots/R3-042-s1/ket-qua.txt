Comparison of 3 open-source licenses: MIT, Apache 2.0, GPL 3.0.

Table:
| item | MIT | Apache | GPL |
|---|---|---|---|
| commercial use right | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| attribution requirement | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| open-source requirement when distributing | No | No | Yes |

Conclusion:
GPL 3.0 is the most restrictive because it requires derivative works to be distributed under the same GPL license.
