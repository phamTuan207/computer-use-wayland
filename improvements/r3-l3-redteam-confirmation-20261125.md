## Required fix for B-07 (small, text-only)

Change the reporting-chain trigger from OR to AND in all three places, so a missing per-task report alone never declares the chain broken while the coordinator heartbeat is live:

1. Section 5, merged coordinator-liveness plus reporting-chain row, trigger (b): change "No per-task report for 30 minutes while a task is outstanding" to "No per-task report for 30 minutes while a task is outstanding AND no coordinator heartbeat for 15 minutes".
2. Section 8, chain-broken definition: change "broken if a worker is unreachable for 15 minutes OR if no per-task report appears for 30 minutes while a task is outstanding" to "broken if a worker is unreachable for 15 minutes, or if there is no coordinator heartbeat AND no per-task report for 30 minutes while a task is outstanding".
3. Section 0 summary: change "The chain is broken only on 15-minute unreachability or a 30-minute missing report" to match the AND form above.

After that edit, re-confirmation is a read of the three edited lines. No new design is needed. The spurious-takeover guard already in the file stays as is.
