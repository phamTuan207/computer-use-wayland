## 3. Queue discipline: ai chay truoc, cho doi the nao

### 3.1 Co che hien co trong code (da co, khong phai suy doan)

Broker chi giu mot Task tai mot thoi diem (scripts/session_remote.py, class Broker, self.task). Cac hanh vi tu choi da co trong code:

- Start khi task dang song: tra `{'ok': False, 'error': 'busy'}` (session_remote.py, ham start, nhannh task ton tai va process con chay hoac state chua finished/failed).
- Start khi con state cu can recovery: tra `recovery_required` (cung ham start, khi snapshot session.json con ton tai hoac _task_recovery_required tra true).
- Request khi mot request khac dang chay: tra `busy`, thong diep 'one request is already running' (session_remote.py, ham _request).
- So sequence sai: `sequence_gap` khi sequence khong phai so tiep theo, `conflict` khi sequence da dung cho request khac, `expired` va `not_found` khi hoi ket qua cua sequence het han hoac khong biet.
- Lease sai: `invalid_lease` khi owner, secret hoac generation khong khop, `broker_changed` khi broker da restart, `lease_file_exists` khi file lease da ton tai, `invalid_lease_file` khi file lease hong. Lease khong co TTL: ham _authorize (session_remote.py dong 778-786) chi kiem tra generation + secret + owner, khong kiem tra thoi gian het han. Lease chi vo hieu khi generation doi (broker restart), secret sai, hoac owner sai. Khong goi la "lease het han".
- Broker khong giu hang doi. Bien start_order la deque lich su 64 lan start gan nhat, khong phai hang doi cho. Viec cho doi la hanh vi phia client tren VM, khong co trong broker.
- Thoi gian cho trong code: IDLE_TIMEOUT 300,0s, START_TIMEOUT 25,0s, REQUEST_TIMEOUT 120,0s, MAX_REQUEST_BYTES 8 MiB (session_remote.py dong 33-39).

### 3.2 Quyet dinh discipline cho step 8 (quyet dinh moi, can dieu phoi duyet)

1. Ai chay truoc: hien tai chi co quy tac nguoi thang duy nhat duoc chap nhan, khong co hang doi, khong co timestamp den cua contender, khong co dam bao thu tu cong bang. Hai worker cung gui start se dua nhau o RLock trong ham _start, nguoi vao critical section truoc thi thang. Cau "first-come-first-served theo lan start duoc broker chap nhan" la dinh nghia vong, khong phai discipline ma code bao dam. FCFS day du giua cac contender, neu dieu phoi muon, la chinh sach moi can implement o phia client hoac them hang doi vao broker, chua co trong code. Khong co muc uu tien trong broker: ham _validate_start (session_remote.py dong 573-585) chi nhan owner, start_id, secret, broker_generation, khong co truong priority nao. "G0 gates finisher co broker priority 1" trong live-test-log.md dong 1778 la cach xep lich chay cua dieu phoi, broker khong thuc thi duoc. Thu tu giua cac track do dieu phoi xep bang lich chay, theo tien le da ghi trong improvements/live-test-log.md dong 1778: cac track lan luot chay va log take/release.
2. Hai worker cung gui start: broker chap nhan mot, worker con lai nhan `busy`. Worker nhan `busy` PHAI CHO, khong duoc fail nhanh va khong duoc mo duong song song. Cach cho: poll `status` (khong can lease) cho den khi status tra state la `finished` hoac `failed`, guardian khong phai `active`, va `recovery: none`, roi start lai voi lease moi (start_id moi). Dieu kien dung KHONG PHAI state `idle`: ham _status tra `idle` chi khi task la None (session_remote.py dong 893), ma self.task chi duoc gan o hai cho, khoi tao None dong 494 va gan task moi dong 667, khong co cho nao gan lai None sau khi task ket thuc. Sau khi owner A finish xong, status tra state `finished`, owner van la owner-a, driver `stopped`, guardian `clean`, va `state == idle` la False (probe tren broker that cua redteam). Vong cho theo spec cu poll den idle se cho den gioi han 300s roi bo cuoc du broker da ranh tu lau. Truoc khi start lai, PHAI xoa (unlink) file lease cu: ham _client_start dung lai cung start_id neu file lease da ton tai, va ham _start tra lai response da ghi cho start_id do tu start_history, nen retry cung start_id se nhan lai `busy` mai du broker dang ranh (probe cua redteam: cung start_id van busy sau khi A finish, start_id moi thi ok). Neu tao lease moi tai cung duong dan khi file cu van con thi loi `lease_file_exists` (ham _write_new_lease dung O_EXCL). Bang chung chay lai sau idle chi o muc chay lai thu cong: speed-proto-pick4-summary.md dong 25 ghi run 1 executor ghi BLOCKED sau mot lan start that bai moi task thay vi retry, dong 94 ghi run 2 la chay lai mot lan sau khi Pick 1 va Pick 2 xong va broker idle thi di 4/4. Vong poll status roi start lai voi lease moi la hanh vi moi can implement va test, chua co bang chung chay that. Live-test-log.md dong 3852 (Pick 4 cho broker thay vi va cham, probe va ca hai arm khong khoi dong, khong mo session nao) la bang chung cho dang khong khoi dong khi broker ban, khong phai bang chung cho vong poll.
3. Gioi han cho: toi da 300s tong cong, la quyet dinh moi lay moc tu IDLE_TIMEOUT, khong phai gioi han co trong code. Khong co vong cho nao trong session_remote.py, ca phia broker va phia client: ham _client_start chi thu start mot lan, nhan busy thi tra ve ngay. IDLE_TIMEOUT 300,0s khong phai gioi han cho cua contender: ham tick dung 300s nay de ket thuc mot task dang chay ma khong co request nao trong 300s (dat finish_requested va stop_reason idle_timeout). Lay 300s lam moc cho gioi han cho la lua chon thiet ke hop ly, nhung phai ghi ro la so moi. Qua 300s van busy thi worker dung cho, ghi log `busy` kem owner hien tai doc tu status, va bao ve dieu phoi. Tong thoi gian cho thuc te co the vuot 300s nhieu lan neu nhieu owner noi nhau, gioi han nay chi co nghia la 300s tong cong roi bao ve dieu phoi. Khoang poll giua cac lan status: UNKNOWN, de xuat 5s, can dieu phoi chot. Khong poll lien tuc kieu busy-loop. Gia tri backoff va jitter cho cuoc dua start lai khi nhieu worker cung poll roi cung start (thundering herd, chi mot thang, so con lai nhan busy roi cho lai) cung chua co, can dieu phoi chot, xem UNKNOWN 13. Worker dang cho chua co lease khi broker restart giua luc cho: doc lai status, chi tao lease moi sau khi cleanup xong va status sach, nhu muc 7 cho truong hop broker_changed luc start.
4. Khong co preemption qua broker API. Khong co API nao de mot client cuop task cua owner khac: ham _authorize tu choi moi request, finish, result neu owner, secret hoac generation khong khop. Chi owner moi finish hoac cancel task cua minh qua broker. Nhung cau "Owner dang chay khong bao gio bi day ra" la qua manh, phai sua: owner van co the bi dung boi cancel latch, idle timeout 300s (ham tick), restart broker (moi lease cu vo hieu voi loi broker_changed), hoac kill process driver/broker. Cancel latch la file $STATE/cancelled, ham _cancelled chi kiem tra file co ton tai hay khong, khong kiem tra owner, nen bat ky ai tao duoc file cancelled deu dung duoc owner. Dieu phoi khong duoc dung latch lam cong cu dung owner: latch la cua rieng nguoi dung, chi duoc xoa bang lenh resume (cancel_hotkey.py:222 va cu.py:3166 la hai cho duy nhat unlink latch), va trong khi latch ton tai moi start deu tra `cancelled` (xem muc 6.3). Dieu phoi ra lenh dung bang cach khac, khong phai bang latch.
5. Trong mot owner, request chay tuan tu tuyet doi. Sequence number bat buoc tang dan, mot request tai mot thoi diem. Request thu hai trong khi request dau chua xong nhan `busy`, khong duoc xep hang trong broker.
6. Retry tren lease contention dung backoff, theo tien le da ghi tai live-test-log.md dong 3701: "Broker is single-owner: retry with backoff on lease contention." Dong do nam trong mot thong bao spawn ke hoach cho 4 coordinator, khong phai mot lan chay da do, nen muc chung minh chi la ghi chu ke hoach. Khong retry ngay lap tuc vong lap kin. Gia tri backoff va jitter cu the chua co, can dieu phoi chot, xem UNKNOWN 13.
7. Xu ly tung loi start cua worker cho:
   - `busy`: cho nhu muc 2 roi unlink file lease cu, start lai voi start_id moi.
   - `lease_file_exists`: unlink file lease cu roi start lai voi start_id moi, khong tao lease moi de len file cu.
   - `recovery_required`: khong retry start, chuyen sang quy trinh recovery o muc 6.3.
   - `broker_changed`: doc status, chi tao lease moi sau khi cleanup xong.
   - `cancelled`: latch dang ton tai, khong start lai. Cho nguoi dung resume (lenh resume xoa file latch), roi moi start lai. Worker hay dieu phoi khong tu xoa latch.
   - `invalid_lease` hoac `lease_owner_mismatch`: dung, khong dung lai lease cu, bao loi ve dieu phoi.
   - `broker_unavailable`: broker chet hoac socket mat, chuyen sang fallback one-shot SSH o muc 6.1 va khoi dong lai relay o muc 6.2.
